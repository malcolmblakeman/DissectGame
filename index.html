Dissect

A shape, already cut into hinged pieces. Every piece is hinged to its neighbour, so it can only swing around its joint — swing them until the outline becomes the target shape.

  • Tap a piece to see how it can move, then tap a handle to swing it.
  • Two handles mean the piece can swing either way — take your pick.
  • A swing carries everything hinged behind the piece, and no piece ever passes through another.
  • The camera pulls back so you can watch each swing. Rotate turns the whole board 90°.
  • Click the level title to play the same dissection the other way round.
  • Hint finds the shortest remaining route from wherever you are.

Eight levels — seven hinged pairs (triangles, squares, pentagons, hexagons and an octagon) plus a shuffled scramble. Click the level title to swap direction, or the level button (bottom-right) to jump around.
